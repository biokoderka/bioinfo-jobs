About the offer

AI BIOLOGICS - RARE AND PROGRESSIVE DISEASE Latch builds rigorous scientific benchmarks ( benchmarks.bio ) for AI agents across biology. We work with frontier labs and pharma to measure whether agents can handle real biological workflows: the messy, multi-layer reasoning that matters in the field. We are seeking exceptional basic and clinical scientists to join our team working at the frontier of what artificial intelligence can achieve in combatting rare and/or progressive diseases. Of note: this role will not include work on cancer, for which we have a separate role. This is an early team, and you will have a critical role in defining our strategy across multiple benchmarks.
